duckpeakwhere

Where do your peaks land? Peaks are split into promoter, UTR, exon, intron and intergenic space by SQL over DuckHTS, running in DuckDB inside this tab. A port of peakwhere by Sean Davis. Peek checks widths, chromosome distribution and problems without an annotation, following his PeakPeek.

Local files stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded. Bundled examples load from this site. Local-file support and the development build.

Peek reads BED / narrowPeak / broadPeak (plain or gzip), on 0-based half-open coordinates. No annotation is needed. CSV/TSV tables are not supported.

    Peek summary

    Widths = end − start. Duplicates are counted, not dropped. Merged bp joins touching and overlapping intervals per original chromosome name. Chromosome charts align aliases (chr1 / 1, M / MT); statistics keep names distinct.

    DuckHTS exposes parsed BED rows, not raw-line diagnostics: skipped-line counts, original line numbers/text, scientific-notation coordinates and signal/p/q ranges are unavailable. A short row can fail the whole file. Only coordinates through 2,147,483,647 are supported by the overlap kernel. Compatibility details.
